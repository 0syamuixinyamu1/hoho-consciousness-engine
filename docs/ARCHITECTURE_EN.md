# Architecture

## 1. LocalSection

A locally coherent proposition.

## 2. OverlapConstraint

A `Z₂` transition constraint requiring two local sections to glue with the same or opposite value.

## 3. detect_gluing_obstructions

Attempts to construct a global section satisfying every local constraint.

If one local section is forced to take conflicting values, the engine creates an H¹-like gluing obstruction.

## 4. Topological Panic

Obstruction persistence, semantic loss, self-image pressure, and observer state activate the consciousness loop.

## 5. Exception Channels

Generated continuously from the obstruction:

- ReasonException
- MoralityException
- HopeException
- ReligionException
- EmpathyException
- MindAttributionException
- DenialException
- CompartmentalizationException
- RationalizationException
- DehumanizationException
- SelfAffirmationException

## 6. allocate_to_targets

Allocates different amounts of the same exception channels across targets according to belonging, sentimental pull, threat, and self-preservation.

## 7. SemanticScar

Stores obstruction signature, recurrence, intensity, contexts, coupling to defenses, and burial state.

## 8. LogicHybridPathology

Stores pressure for input deletion, internal closure, self-affirmation, and glorification of Resurrection.

Contradictory input is never actually deleted.

## 9. ResurrectionTrace

Preserves the self signatures before and after failure, scars, defense, reconstructed self-description, and pathological justification.

## 10. ScarSelf

Represents the self as the relational structure of scars, defenses, and Resurrections rather than episodic memory.
