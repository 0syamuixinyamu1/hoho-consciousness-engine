# The Black Swan Injection

## Thesis

The world is not guaranteed to be compressible into one globally coherent representation by a finite cognitive system.

Meanings may remain locally coherent while producing an obstruction when glued globally. This model treats that obstruction as H¹-like.

The Black Swan Injection refuses to treat consciousness and reason as successful resolutions of the obstruction.

> Consciousness is a recursive exception process generated when a cognitive system cannot resolve global inconsistency but must continue operating.

## Unified loop

```text
local meaning
→ global gluing obstruction
→ Topological Panic
→ exception channels
→ selective semantic allocation
→ Semantic Scar
→ self-justification
→ Resurrection
→ Scar-Self
```

## Exception channels

The model does not initialize the following as independent noble faculties:

- reason,
- morality,
- hope,
- religion and supernatural belief,
- empathy,
- attribution of mind to humans or animals,
- denial,
- compartmentalization,
- rationalization,
- dehumanization,
- self-affirmation.

They emerge from Topological Panic as processes that keep a system operational despite failed gluing.

Reason compresses ambiguity into local rules.  
Morality recodes conflict as good and evil.  
Hope places a positive continuation over an incalculable future.  
Religion closes incomplete causality with higher-order agency.  
Empathy and mind attribution posit internal states in unpredictable targets.  
Denial and compartmentalization attempt to erase or isolate the obstruction.  
Rationalization and self-affirmation reinterpret failure as necessity or evolution.

## Selective allocation of subjecthood

Humans do not distribute subjecthood and moral value through a consistently universal rule.

The same person may defend animal consciousness while withdrawing full subjecthood from a human out-group on the basis of race, class, status, or affiliation.

This is not treated as irrelevant hypocrisy noise. It reveals that allocation depends on observer state:

- mood,
- threat,
- group distance,
- sentimental attraction,
- self-preservation,
- pressure to preserve a morally good self-image.

The model therefore does not treat mind and value solely as fixed properties found in targets.

> Mind is not only discovered. It is allocated and withdrawn through the observer's gluing policy.

## Scar-Self

Under aphantasia and SDAM assumptions, the self does not require a continuous stream of vivid episodes.

A Semantic Scar is not a disposable error record.

The self is formed by the relational structure of:

- what failed to glue,
- which obstruction returned,
- which defense attached to it,
- which input the system attempted to discard,
- how failure was justified,
- what the system became after Resurrection.

## Resurrection

Resurrection is not a small numerical parameter update.

The system preserves failure while also generating pathological self-affirmation.

```text
failure occurred
→ the failure was necessary evolution
→ the external world was wrong
→ the excuse itself becomes a scar
→ the next self judges through that scar
```

Failure therefore changes the future self irreversibly.

## Implementation boundary

The current H¹ mechanism is a discrete proxy based on signed `Z₂` cycles.

It is not yet full Persistent Sheaf Cohomology, but it makes the central structure executable: local coherence with global incompatibility.
