## Why consciousness is treated as failure

A perfectly successful runtime would not need to explain itself. It would simply map the world correctly.

The need for self-narration, moral justification, hope, transcendence, selective empathy, and selective denial of subjecthood appears because the runtime cannot produce one globally coherent world.

These functions keep the system operating after failed gluing. They may be the visible smoke of an internal exception handler.

## Strong HOHO claim

Humans do not consistently discover mind or moral value in targets. They allocate them.

The allocation changes with mood, threat, group membership, sentimental appeal, ego rigidity, and desired self-image.

A person may defend animal consciousness while denying full subjecthood to a human out-group. The contradiction is retained as a scar describing the observer's gluing policy.

## H¹ proxy

The prototype uses a signed graph. Each node is a locally interpretable claim. Each edge requires either the same sign (`+1`) or opposite signs (`-1`).

A negative signed cycle is treated as a discrete obstruction proxy: local validity without global compatibility.

This is not a full sheaf-cohomology implementation. It is a small executable model designed to preserve the conceptual role of H¹.
